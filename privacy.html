Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Social Media Management Tool ("the Application") handles and protects your data when you connect your accounts, including TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Application to publish or manage content, we may access the following data via authorized APIs:

  • Profile Information: Basic account details (such as username, display name, and user ID) via TikTok's user.info.basic scope to verify creator status and permissions.
  • Media Content: Video and audio files that you intentionally select and provide for publishing.
  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens necessary to perform actions on your behalf.

2. How We Use Your Information

All accessed data is used solely to provide and operate the publishing features:

  • Authenticating your identity with TikTok and supported platforms.
  • Querying creator upload requirements (such as privacy level and allowed interaction settings).
  • Uploading and publishing your requested video content to your profile.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: The Application operates locally. Your API tokens, client secrets, and configuration files are stored exclusively on your local machine (e.g., in a local .env configuration).
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal information or media files to any third-party servers, data brokers, or analytics providers.
  • Secure Transmission: All communications with TikTok servers are conducted directly over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • No Remote Database: We do not maintain external database servers holding your personal content or account history.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke the Application's access at any time directly through your TikTok account settings (Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions) or by deleting the local .env configuration file on your machine.

5. Compliance with Platform Policies

The Application strictly adheres to the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and API policies regarding user privacy and data security.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact the developer directly.
